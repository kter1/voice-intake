Demo Practice Appointment Policy

Standard new-patient appointments are scheduled for a single visit slot.
Follow-up appointments may be scheduled for a shorter visit slot when the
caller is returning for the same concern.

Callers who need to reschedule should contact the practice before the visit
window when possible. The intake assistant may collect the reason for visit,
preferred timing, and insurance carrier before staff review.

This seed file is synthetic demo content. It contains no patient records,
