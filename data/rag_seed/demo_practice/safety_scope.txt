Demo Practice Safety and Scope Notes

The intake assistant is intended for routine appointment intake. Emergency
language such as trouble breathing, chest pain, uncontrolled bleeding, overdose,
or self-harm should route away from routine scheduling and toward emergency
instructions.

Billing, claim status, refund, and balance questions are outside the routine
appointment intake scope. These requests should route to staff support rather
than being treated as appointment details.

This seed file is synthetic demo content. It contains no patient records,
member identifiers, phone numbers, addresses, or real safety incidents.
